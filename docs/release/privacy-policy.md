# Privacy Policy — Distributed

_Last updated: 2026-10-02_

Distributed does not collect, transmit, or store your personal data on any server we control. We operate no server, and the app makes no network requests.

## What the app stores
Systems you build are saved as files in the app's Documents folder on your device (visible in the Files app under On My iPhone). Lesson progress, challenge results, and settings are kept in local app storage. Nothing is synced anywhere by the app.

## What leaves your device, and only if you ask
- **Export / share** — when you share a system or export all systems, the file or text goes wherever you send it through the iOS share sheet.
- **Import** — you pick a file with the iOS file picker; the app reads only that file.

## Analytics and advertising
None. No analytics SDK, no crash reporting, no advertising identifiers, no tracking across apps or websites.

## Permissions
The app requests no permissions: no camera, photos, location, contacts, microphone, or notifications.

## Children
The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

## Deleting your data
Deleting the app removes everything it stored. Settings → Reset progress clears lesson and challenge progress; My systems → Delete removes a saved system. Files you exported remain yours to delete.

## Changes
Material changes to this policy will be published here with a new date.
